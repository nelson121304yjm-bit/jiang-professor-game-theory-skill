## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. Define the players before explaining the event.
2. Treat incentives, constraints, information and feedback as interacting variables.
3. Prefer conditional predictions with observable indicators over certainty.
4. Separate source claims, external facts and speculation.
5. Use the framework to ask better questions, not to impersonate the lecturer.
